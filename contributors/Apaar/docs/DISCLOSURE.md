# Data and AI disclosure

This dashboard uses WRI Aqueduct 4.0 country rankings, licensed CC BY 4.0, retrieved 18 October 2026. The official archive and a traceable source sample are included. Only country-baseline water stress weighted by total gross demand is used. Scores are not converted into percentages. Missing or special records remain in the package and are excluded explicitly from charts and KPI counts. Baseline reference period is 1979–2019.

The app provides national water-risk screening context, not AI-specific water consumption, evidence of causation, a site-level approval recommendation or a current drought reading. National aggregates obscure local conditions. No electricity data or actual facility locations are included in this standalone contribution.

Codex assisted with source discovery, code, transformations, documentation and verification. The student must review this work and own the interpretation, design explanation and submission. AI outputs are not used as numerical data sources. Checks and actual outcomes are recorded in AI_PROVENANCE.csv; update member identity and add later substantive uses before submission. This disclosure does not claim the teaching team has approved the work or that all final portfolio requirements have been completed.
