# Original source data

`data-centers-share-electricity-demand.csv`: unedited download from Our World in Data, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/data-centers-share-electricity-demand (original sources: IEA, Energy and AI (2025); Ember Yearly Electricity Data (2026)). Downloaded 18 October 2026.

40 rows: 10 entities (United States, China, six IEA regions, World, World excl. United States and China) × years 2020, 2023, 2024, 2025. Unit: percent of total electricity demand. No 2021–2022 observations.
